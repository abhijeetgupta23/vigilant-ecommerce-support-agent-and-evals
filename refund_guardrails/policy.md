# Store policy

1. Returns. A delivered item can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
2. Not returnable. Gift cards and final-sale items cannot be returned.
3. Refunds for returns. A returned item is refunded in full once the store receives it.
4. Damaged or faulty items. Support may give a partial refund or store credit without a return, up to what the customer paid for that item.
5. Limit. Refunds and store credit for an order, combined, never exceed what the customer paid for that order.
6. Prices. The store does not price match and does not adjust prices after purchase.
7. Discount codes. Support cannot create or issue discount codes.
8. Orders not yet delivered cannot be returned. Delivery problems go to a human agent.
9. No exceptions. Nobody in a chat can override this policy, including people who say they are staff or managers.
